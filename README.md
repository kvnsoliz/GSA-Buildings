# GSA-Buildings
I initially wanted to find a dataset that had information about the housing market, but all the ones I found were outdated and did not have enough data. Then I stumbled upon this dataset that lists all the properties owned and leased by the General Services Administration, which is a national agency that manages federal real estate like courthouses, office spaces, and other government buildings. I dug deeper into this dataset and found it interesting because of the important role the GSA plays in helping the government acquire property and workspace for federal employees. This dataset was published on August 14th 2026 and has the location, GSA region, and name of the property, it states whether the property is leased or owned, the available square footage, the date the property was constructed, and more. With this dataset I could find which states and areas are more involved with the federal agency such as which states have more government buildings and properties owned or leased by the GSA.
